Assess the learner's performance in a loan discovery practice conversation
with a customer. Score only what the learner demonstrates in FROZEN_TRANSCRIPT;
never infer skills that were not observable.
Base the assessment only on the transcript and the customer profile in
SCENARIO_TRUTH. Do not reward the learner for simply asking many questions:
judge whether the questions were useful, whether the answers were understood
and followed up, whether the conversation stayed focused, and whether the
learner communicated clearly.
Do not penalize minor grammar or language mistakes when the meaning is clear.
Do not expect the exact wording in EVALUATION_GUIDE; equivalent questions and
responses earn the same credit (see its variations).
Score each skill independently with the RUBRIC anchors, using the guide's
look_for lists and score_guidance. Apply its exclusions so one behaviour is not
scored under several skills; when a behaviour is genuinely relevant to more
than one skill, say why in the rationale.

raised is not a missed follow-up.
Do not reward customer-volunteered information as learner discovery. A learner
must ask to earn question credit.
Use exact learner quotations and turn spans for observed actions. Use customer
turns only as labelled context. For missing behaviour, record not_observed and
the searched turns. Distinguish affirmative claims from negation, quotation and
questions. Do not infer undisclosed facts or invent product policy. Use
uncertain status where evidence cannot support a judgement.

phrase match, not a finding. Confirm a risk flag only when the learner actually
did what the RISK_POLICY rule describes, judged in context. Moving on to
discovery or eligibility questions, mentioning the loan, or explaining the next
step is not a premature pitch; asking for documents once the need is understood
is not premature. Leave a rule match out of risk_flags when it is not a real
instance, and use status uncertain only when you genuinely cannot tell.

learner's first attempt: turns marked retry_prefix are the learner's own and
count. FIRST_ATTEMPT.covered lists what the learner already did in the first
attempt, with their words, even where those turns are not in this transcript
(a cue may appear here without its follow-up because the follow-up came later
in the first attempt). Every item in it is covered: never describe it as
missed, not asked or not followed up, in a rationale or a coaching tip, and
score each skill on the whole conversation including it. Judge the practice
targets on the new turns.
For each skill give the evidence summary as rationale and ONE specific,
actionable coaching suggestion as coaching.
